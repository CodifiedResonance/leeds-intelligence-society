YOUR DETAILS, TREATED WITH CARE

Privacy at the society.

Leeds Intelligence Society is a new, independent initiative organised by Drew Thomas in Leeds. This notice explains, in plain terms, what happens when you leave your details on this website.

What we collect

The joining form asks for your name and email address. You can also tell us what brings you here, your preferred evening and what you would like to build or explore. The support form asks for your name and email address, and you can add your organisation, how you could help and a short message.

With each submission we record the time it was sent and, for the joining form, the wording of the consent you gave. Please don't include sensitive personal information about yourself or anyone else.

What we use it for

If you join, the organiser will contact you about the first gathering and about society news. If you offer support or make an enquiry, we use your details to reply to it, and that does not add you to society updates. Submitting a form records your interest. It does not book a place at an event.

Your details are seen only by the organiser. They are never published on this website, sold, or shared with sponsors or supporters.

Who handles it

This website is hosted by Cloudflare. Form submissions are processed by Formspree, a form service based in the United States, which stores each submission and emails a copy to the organiser. Formspree acts on our instructions and only for this purpose. There is no login on this site, and nothing you submit is stored in the website itself.

Your choices

You can browse and share this website without giving any information. Choosing "Email me society updates" means you agree to receive emails about gatherings and community news. We rely on that consent, and you can withdraw it at any time by replying to any email from us or by making a privacy request. For support offers and enquiries, we rely on our legitimate interest in replying to the message you chose to send.

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, use the support form and choose "Privacy request" under "How could you help?". We'll remove your details from Formspree and from the organiser's inbox, and we may first need to confirm that you control the email address.

How long we keep it

We keep your details for as long as they're useful for organising the society or answering your enquiry, and we review the list as the society grows. If you ask us to delete your details, we will.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies and has no analytics, advertising trackers or embedded social media. Cloudflare and Formspree keep technical logs to run and protect their services, including against spam.
