1How we can help

1.1

Discuss a proposal. The author can talk through a paper’s reasoning, its evidence and the decisions it asks for, by phone, by video or in person in Leeds.

1.2

A briefing. A short written or spoken briefing on a paper, prepared for a particular meeting, consultation or deadline. Tell us the date and we will work to it.

1.3

Evidence and corrections. If you hold data that fills one of a paper’s gaps, or you think a figure is wrong, please tell us. Corrections are made openly and recorded in the paper’s version history.

1.4

Citing a paper. Every paper is published under a Creative Commons Attribution licence and can be quoted and reused with credit. Each paper gives a citation on its cover. If you cite one in a report, consultation response or debate, please tell us where, so we can link to it.

1.5

Leeds Intelligence Society is independent. It is not part of any council, university, party or company, and has received no funding from any organisation named in its papers.
