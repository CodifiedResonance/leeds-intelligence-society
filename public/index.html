01 / THE IDEA

What becomes possible
when we build
together?

Intelligence is opening new doors. We want to walk through them together, and see what Leeds can make on the other side.

Leeds Intelligence Society is a free, in-person society for people working with frontier AI. We meet to make things, test ideas and carry the good ones further. It is a room where a conversation can become a prototype, and a prototype can become something that matters.

We are starting here, with an open door and a serious ambition: that the next era should be something we help shape, not something that simply happens to us.

Human curiosity.
New kinds of intelligence.
Shared possibility.

A CITY OF MAKERS, ON THE RECORD

Leeds has done this before.

  1. 1758

    The Middleton Railway becomes the first railway authorised by an Act of Parliament.

  2. 1767

  3. 1812

    Matthew Murray's Salamanca runs on the Middleton line, the first steam locomotive to work commercially.

  4. 1884

    Michael Marks opens a penny stall in Kirkgate Market. Marks & Spencer begins.

  5. 1888

    Louis Le Prince films a garden in Roundhay and traffic on Leeds Bridge, among the first moving pictures ever made.

A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Before you
come along.

Do I need to be a developer?+

No. Bring curiosity and a willingness to contribute. Designers, makers, researchers, founders, people with deep local knowledge and people exploring their very first idea all have a place here.

What should I bring?+

A laptop if you have one, an idea if you have one, and something you're curious about. You're just as welcome to come and listen, pair up with someone and find your way into a project.

When and where is the first gathering?+

We're finding a central Leeds home now. The plan is a weekday evening, roughly every three weeks. The first date, venue and access details will be shared once confirmed. Joining the list helps us shape it around the people who are coming.

Will it always be free?+

Yes. Free participation is a founding commitment. We're looking for venue partners and supporters to cover the practical costs, so a ticket price never decides who gets to be in the room.

Do I need to be from Leeds?+

No. Lifelong Loiners, recent arrivals and people who travel in from across Yorkshire are all welcome. The gatherings are in Leeds, and some of the work will be for Leeds.

Does everything have to be about Leeds?+

No. Bring your own project or explore something completely new. Alongside that open space, we'll develop work that helps the city: practical tools, better evidence and well-tested ideas for its future.

Who is behind it?+

Leeds Intelligence Society was started by Drew Thomas and is at its founding stage. The intention is for a small group of contributors to emerge through the work and look after the society together.

Is this part of the council or a university?+

No, it's independent. The longer ambition is to offer useful work and well-tested proposals to Leeds, developed in conversation with the people they affect.
